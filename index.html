Step 01

Site inspection

Physical visit, geo-tagged photos, access, surroundings, construction quality and stage.

Step 02

Measurement & documents

Plot and built-up area measured and checked against sanctioned plans and title documents.

Step 03

Market & income analysis

Comparable sales, guidance values and — for businesses — cash flows and asset schedules.

Step 04

Valuation report

A clear, signed report with method, workings and value conclusion — ready for the bank or client.

Valuation methods

How the number
is built.

M.01

Market approach

Comparable sales and current market rates, adjusted for location, access, frontage and legal status.

M.02

Land & building

Land value plus depreciated replacement cost of the structure — the workhorse for lending valuations.

M.03

Discounted cash flow

Projected income discounted to present value — for income-producing property and company valuation.

M.04

Residual method

Gross development value minus costs and developer's margin — for land with development potential.
