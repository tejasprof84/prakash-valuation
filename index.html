03 / Method

How the number
is built.

M.01

Market approach

Comparable sales and current market rates, adjusted for location, access, frontage and legal status.

M.02

Land & building

Land value plus depreciated replacement cost of the structure — the workhorse for lending valuations.

M.03

Discounted cash flow

Projected income discounted to present value — for income-producing property and company valuation.

M.04

Residual method

Gross development value minus costs and developer's margin — for land with development potential.
